# Embedded fonts

Orbit bundles the regular IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono typefaces for
consistent native rendering on Windows and Linux.

IBM Plex is Copyright IBM Corp. and is licensed under the SIL Open Font
License, Version 1.1. The complete license text is available at
<https://scripts.sil.org/OFL> and in the upstream IBM Plex repository:
<https://github.com/IBM/plex/tree/master/packages/plex-sans>.
